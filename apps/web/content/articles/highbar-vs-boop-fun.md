Highbar and Boop.fun can both be described as Meteora launch paths, and that is usually where a useful comparison should stop being casual. The fee split, the graduation figures, and the launch surface are different products. This comparison uses the [Highbar docs](/docs) and Boop's official [deployment](https://docs.boop.fun/token-deployment-101), [bonding curve](https://docs.boop.fun/the-boop-bonding-curve), [graduation](https://docs.boop.fun/graduation) and [rewards](https://docs.boop.fun/rewards) pages, checked on 16 November 2026. Highbar is our product. Where Boop's official design is the better fit for a job, this article says so.

## At a glance

| | Boop.fun (official pages) | Highbar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch | Free website form, or by tagging its bot on X | Website form at [Launch](/launch) |
| Graduation | Official pages give both ~86 SOL on the curve and ~400 SOL market cap | 85, 150 or 250 SOL, chosen at launch |
| After graduation | Liquidity moves to a Meteora pool | Locked forever in two DAMM v2 positions |
| Post-graduation fee | 2%, with 62.5% to holders or delegates | 1% DAMM v2 fee |
| Creator share after graduation | Not listed as a 0.6%-style deployer cut of that 2% table | 0.6% to 0.7% of volume with the LP rebate |
| Pre-graduation fees | Official deployment page: all fees to the platform | 1% on the curve; creator 0.4% to 0.6% |
| Holder rewards / delegation | Yes, plus optional X-account delegation | Not documented |
| Anti-sniper option | Not described on the official pages cited here | Optional 25% to about 1% over 60 seconds |

## Graduation figures, left unresolved

A comparison that picked one Boop number and ignored the other official page would be neater and less accurate. The bonding-curve page describes about 86 SOL accumulated on the curve. The graduation page describes a market cap of about 400 SOL, 80 SOL migrated with remaining tokens, and 6 SOL to the team, plus a note that the curve needs about 80 SOL and 206,900,000 tokens. Highbar's targets are simpler because they are a single published tier table: 85, 150 or 250 SOL. That is a documentation difference, not a quality score.

Official Boop supply language also differs: deployment docs describe about 20% for the Meteora pool, 0.5% to stakers and 4.5% burned; the rewards page describes a 5% staker airdrop. Highbar sends 20% of supply, plus raised SOL minus the documented migration fee, into two locked DAMM v2 positions. See [Meteora DAMM v2 explained](/articles/meteora-damm-v2-explained-token-graduation) for that venue.

## Fees and who they are for

If you want holders (or a delegated X account) to receive 62.5% of a 2% post-graduation fee, official Boop pages document that design and Highbar does not. If you want a published creator share of 0.4% to 0.6% on the curve and 0.6% to 0.7% after graduation, Highbar documents that design and Boop's 2% table does not. Before graduation, official Boop docs send all fees to the platform; Highbar pays the creator on the curve from the first trades. Those are opposite answers to the same early-volume question.

The official Boop graduation page, on the day this was checked, also marked graduation rewards as paused as the Temple of Moon takes over. Re-read that page before you treat a reward as current. Holder-reward design, paused or not, is not a safety feature. Locked Highbar liquidity is not a safety feature either.

## Launch surface

Boop can be launched from its website or from X, which is a real product difference. Highbar's form is on the website, and the [Highbar Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oioebamfkpahdnjkbbmldepkackabhed) can launch or trade from an X post only after you have already chosen Highbar; it is not a Boop-style tag-to-create flow. Creator pre-buy on Boop is described in two official ways — up to 50% at creation on the deployment page, or all 74.32% on the curve in the FAQ, which would trigger an immediate graduation — so check the live form. Highbar allows an optional dev buy of up to 20 SOL in the launch transaction.

## Who each one suits

Boop suits people who want holder rewards or fee delegation and who will read both official graduation figures. Highbar suits people who want a chosen SOL tier, a creator share on both phases, and locked DAMM v2 liquidity, and who accept a small, new venue. They overlap only as Meteora-related launch paths. For the wider Meteora set, see [Meteora launchpad platforms compared](/articles/meteora-launchpad-platforms-compared). Read Boop's official pages and Highbar's [docs](/docs) before you choose, and use [Launch](/launch) only if Highbar is the product you actually want.

Figures in this article were checked on 16 November 2026 against the official pages linked above.

Those pages change, so re-read them before you launch or trade, and do not treat this guide as a substitute for the live docs.

If a figure here disagrees with an official page, the official page is the one that binds.

Highbar is a small, new launchpad. Nothing in this article is a reason to buy or sell a token, and no launchpad can guarantee demand.

Locked liquidity and a creator fee share are design choices. They do not make a token safe, and they do not stop sniper bots on their own.

Always confirm fee rates, graduation targets and liquidity rules on the live docs, or on the on-chain config, immediately before you sign.

A useful way to use a comparison like this is to write down the three rules you actually care about — the curve fee, the creator share, and what happens to liquidity at graduation — and check those three lines on each official page, rather than choosing a launchpad by name or by a social-media clip.

If you are launching, prepare the token name, ticker, image and any links first, then read the fee table for the exact config you are about to sign, including whether an anti-sniper or app fee applies. If you are trading, check the mint, the pool, and the live fee on the confirmation screen.
