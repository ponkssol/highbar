Fee comparison tables go stale the moment a product ships a new official-app surcharge. This one is a snapshot of official pages checked on 16 November 2026, not a living rate sheet and not a ranking. Each row cites the kind of fee it describes, because a 1% curve fee and a 2.5% in-app fee are not the same object. For creator-share mechanics, see [memecoin creator fees](/articles/memecoin-creator-fees-solana-launchpad). For pump.fun's official-app surcharge, see [pump.fun fees explained](/articles/pump-fun-fees-explained). For LaunchLab's layered shares, see [Raydium LaunchLab fees explained](/articles/raydium-launchlab-fees-explained).

## Official trader-facing fees, by phase

| Product | On the curve, or before the threshold | After graduation or bonding | Extra official-app or extension fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pump.fun | 1.25% (0.30% creator, 0.95% protocol, 0% LP) | PumpSwap canonical tiers 1.250% to 0.300% | Platform Fee 0.45% or 0.40% in official apps, not in PumpSwap or the Terminal |
| Highbar | 1% (Meteora 0.2% of the trade; creator 0.4%–0.6% by tier) | 1% DAMM v2 fee; creator 0.6%–0.7% with rebate | No website app fee; Chrome extension 0.02% of the SOL amount |
| Jupiter Studio | 1% (meme) or 1%/2% (custom), 50/50 with Jupiter | Same fee, still 50/50, for life | No separate app tier in the official user docs cited here; optional anti-sniper starts at 99% for 1–5 minutes |
| Raydium LaunchLab | Default 1% curve fee, with official protocol, platform and creator layers | Usual 0.25% CPMM fee | Launch and graduation network costs are documented separately (~0.115 SOL launch, ~0.04 SOL graduation) |
| Bags, Default SOL mode | 2%, 1% platform / 1% gross claimers | 2%, with 25% compounding | Other official modes range from 0.25% to 10% |
| Float | Official choice of 1%, 2% or 2.5%; Raydium keeps 0.25% on the curve | Follow Float's live official docs for the post-graduation split | First-buy cap and ticker-relaunch fees are documented separately |
| Moonshot Create | 0.5% dynamic coin fee before bonding | 0.3% after bonding at a $1 million market cap | Official app 2.5% ($2–$100, about a $0.99 minimum) or 1% ($100+) |
| Boop.fun | Official deployment page: all fees to the platform; no pre-graduation percentage stated | 2%, with 62.5% to holders or delegates | Not described as a separate official-app surcharge on the pages cited here |
| Heaven | 1% protocol fee below $100k market cap, plus a 0.1% or 1% creator fee by category | Stays on Heaven's AMM; protocol fee becomes 0.25% or 0.5% above $100k | Official Pro Pools table lists a 6-second linear decay on standard pools |
| StonkFun | Official developer docs: 1% LaunchLab fee, 0.5% forwarded to the creator | Follows LaunchLab's CPMM path; usual 0.25% CPMM fee per Raydium | Optional 1% or 3% transfer tax on official v3 reward launches |
| LetsBonk / bonk.fun | Public Raydium platform configs checked 16 November 2026 show 1.25% or 1% fee rates and a 0 creator fee rate | Follows the LaunchLab / Raydium path of the chosen config | This article does not invent a LetsBonk official-app surcharge |

Rows are not ranked. A 0.25% post-graduation CPMM fee is cheaper for a trader than Highbar's 1% DAMM v2 fee, on that phase only. A Moonshot official-app trade at 2.5% can be more expensive than pump.fun's 1.25% curve fee, even though the coin fee on Moonshot is 0.5% before bonding. Always add the anti-sniper window when it is on.

## Official creator shares, in one place

These are also not earnings forecasts:

- Pump.fun: 0.30% of each curve trade.
- Highbar: 0.4%–0.6% on the curve, 0.6%–0.7% after graduation with the rebate.
- Jupiter Studio: 50% of a 1% or 2% fee, for life (0.5% or 1% of the trade).
- Moonshot: up to 0.25% pre-bond, up to 0.15% post-bond, paid hourly above $1.
- StonkFun: 0.5% automatically forwarded, per official developer docs.
- Bags: the "Creator" column is the gross claimers pool; on v2 SOL configs the deployer takes 25% of that pool.
- Boop: the largest official post-graduation slice goes to holders or delegates, not to a Highbar-style deployer cut.
- Heaven: 0.1% or 1%, subject to the official review.
- LetsBonk public configs cited above: a 0 creator fee rate.
- Float: official standard creator share is 0% on the current docs checked for this batch; do not rely on older secondary write-ups that described a different split.

## How to use the table

Pick the phase you are in (curve, official app, or graduated pool), open the official page in the same row, and compare that line with the confirmation screen. If a figure here disagrees with the official page, the official page wins. Highbar's full table lives in the [docs](/docs). The [launch page](/launch) shows the live tier. The [Highbar Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oioebamfkpahdnjkbbmldepkackabhed) adds 0.02% only on extension trades. Nothing in this comparison is a reason to buy or sell a token, and no fee is a safety rating.

Figures in this article were checked on 16 November 2026 against the official pages linked above.

Those pages change, so re-read them before you launch or trade, and do not treat this guide as a substitute for the live docs.

If a figure here disagrees with an official page, the official page is the one that binds.

Highbar is a small, new launchpad. Nothing in this article is a reason to buy or sell a token, and no launchpad can guarantee demand.

Locked liquidity and a creator fee share are design choices. They do not make a token safe, and they do not stop sniper bots on their own.

Always confirm fee rates, graduation targets and liquidity rules on the live docs, or on the on-chain config, immediately before you sign.

A useful way to use a comparison like this is to write down the three rules you actually care about — the curve fee, the creator share, and what happens to liquidity at graduation — and check those three lines on each official page, rather than choosing a launchpad by name or by a social-media clip.
