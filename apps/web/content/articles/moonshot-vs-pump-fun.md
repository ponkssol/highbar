Moonshot Create and pump.fun are often searched as alternatives and rarely line up as substitutes. This comparison uses Moonshot's official [Create](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create), [bonding](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create/bonding), [creator fees](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create/creator-fees) and [trade fees](https://support.moonshot.com/trade/fees) pages, and pump.fun's [fees page](https://pump.fun/docs/fees), all checked on 16 November 2026. Neither product is affiliated with Highbar. Highbar appears only at the end as a third, SOL-paired Meteora option.

## Threshold and venue

Moonshot's bonding rule is explicit: a Create coin starts bonding at a $1 million market cap and moves from a virtual bonding-curve pool to a complete liquidity pool. The bonding page also points at Meteora's DBC documentation for background. Pump.fun stays on its own curve until it graduates to PumpSwap. Pump.fun's official fees page lists a 0.015 SOL graduation fee and does not, in the pages used here, state a graduation-threshold market cap or SOL target. This article leaves that number out rather than guess.

If you wanted a $1 million app-bonding event, pump.fun is not a rename of that rule. If you wanted PumpSwap, Moonshot is not a rename of PumpSwap.

## Fees, including the app layer on both sides

On the coin itself, Moonshot's 0.5% pre-bond fee is lower than pump.fun's 1.25% curve fee, and Moonshot's documented creator share (0.25% pre-bond, up to 0.15% after bonding, paid hourly above $1) is different from pump.fun's 0.30% creator share of the curve fee. After bonding, Moonshot's coin fee is 0.3%. After graduation, PumpSwap uses official canonical fee tiers from 1.250% down to 0.300%; this article does not flatten those tiers into one number.

Both official apps add a second fee. Moonshot's trade-fees page lists 2.5% or 1% by trade size, plus network and routing costs. Pump.fun's Platform Fee, from 17 October 2026, is 0.45% or 0.40% in official apps and is not charged in PumpSwap or the Terminal. A trader has to read the confirmation screen on whichever app they use. Our [pump.fun fees explainer](/articles/pump-fun-fees-explained) covers that Platform Fee in more detail, and [pump.fun alternatives](/articles/pump-fun-alternatives-compare-solana-launchpads) places both products in a wider list.

Highbar is not a Moonshot app and not a pump.fun clone. There is no extra website app fee. The [Highbar Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oioebamfkpahdnjkbbmldepkackabhed) adds 0.02% of the SOL amount on extension trades.
