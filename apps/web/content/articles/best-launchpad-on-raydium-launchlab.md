"Best Raydium LaunchLab launchpad" is a reasonable search and a bad way to pick a product, because LaunchLab is a program with several front ends, not a single website with one fee table. The honest version of the article is a comparison of official rules, with a "best for" line per front end and no overall winner. This piece uses [Raydium's LaunchLab docs](https://docs.raydium.io/products/launchlab/overview) and [creation guide](https://docs.raydium.io/user-flows/creating-a-launchlab-token), the public [platform list](https://launch-mint-v1.raydium.io/main/platforms?page=1&pageSize=100), [Float's docs](https://www.floatlaunch.xyz/docs) and StonkFun's [developer docs](https://www.stonkfun.xyz/developers), checked on 16 November 2026.

## What every LaunchLab launch shares

Tokens trade on a Raydium bonding curve and graduate to a Raydium CPMM pool. Fees are layered (protocol, platform, creator). Raydium lists 1% as the default curve fee and 0.25% as the usual CPMM fee after graduation, which is the shared trader-facing advantage of this program. LP is burned and/or locked to the platform. After that, the front end decides the rest.

## Raydium's own page

On raydium.io you can use **JustSendit**, a quick launch with a fixed 85 SOL target and no vesting, or **LaunchLab mode**, a longer setup with a custom target (24 SOL minimum), 51–80% of supply on the curve, and optional vesting. One-time costs are about 0.115 SOL to launch and about 0.04 SOL at graduation. This is the best fit if you want Raydium's own flow, especially vesting, and you are comfortable without a third-party front end's audience. See our [LaunchLab review](/articles/raydium-launchlab-review).

## Bonk.fun (LetsBonk)

The letsbonk.fun configs we checked showed a 1.25% or 1% platform fee rate and a 0 creator fee rate, with almost all LP burned on the 1.25% configs. After graduation, the usual 0.25% CPMM fee applies. Best for traders who want that low post-graduation fee and a well-known LaunchLab front end. A weaker fit for creators who need a published curve share. We could not find an official Bonk.fun fee handbook; the public config is the source used here.

## StonkFun

StonkFun's developer docs describe LaunchLab launches that can be paired with many quote tokens, and say half of the pool's 1% fee — 0.5% of every trade — is forwarded to the creator automatically. Reward launches can add a 1% or 3% transfer tax. Best for creators who want a LaunchLab launch with an official 0.5% creator forward, or a stock/custom quote, and who will read the rewards and revenue pages. See [how to launch on StonkFun](/articles/how-to-launch-on-stonkfun).

## Float

Float also supports stock and other pairs, with a 1%, 2% or 2.5% fee chosen at launch, a 0% creator share on standard coins, a builder launch if you want a personal share, a 2% first-buy cap, ticker relaunch fees, and liquidity locked with Raydium Burn & Earn at about a $150,000 market cap. Best for people who specifically want Float's pair list and fee split, including the $FLOAT buyback on standard coins. Not best if you expected a standard launch to pay the creator. The program has not had an outside audit yet, which Float discloses itself.

## Where Highbar belongs on this list

Highbar is not a LaunchLab front end, so it cannot win a "best LaunchLab launchpad" crown, and this article will not pretend otherwise. It is the documented alternative if you decide the program itself is not what you want: a Meteora DBC, a 1% curve fee, a published creator share by tier, and locked DAMM v2 liquidity at 85, 150 or 250 SOL. The optional anti-sniper fee does not stop bots. Read the [docs](/docs) and the [launch page](/launch), or use the [Highbar Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oioebamfkpahdnjkbbmldepkackabhed).

## How to use this without picking a "winner"

Write down three rules you care about: the curve fee, the creator share, and the quote asset. Then tick them off on each official page, including the live LaunchLab config. If stock pairs are on the list, Highbar and a typical JustSendit launch are already out. If a non-zero creator curve share is on the list, the letsbonk.fun configs we checked and Float's standard coin are already out. That method is slower than a ranked list, and it is the only method this article will stand behind.

## Bottom line

There is no single best LaunchLab launchpad. Raydium's own page is best for vesting and a first-party flow; Bonk.fun is the usual front end with a 0 creator rate in the configs we checked; StonkFun documents a 0.5% creator forward and custom quotes; Float is the paired-asset product with a 0% standard-coin creator share. Pick the official rule set, not the loudest name.

Figures in this article were checked on 16 November 2026 against the official pages linked above.

Those pages change, so re-read them before you launch or trade, and do not treat this guide as a substitute for the live docs.

If a figure here disagrees with an official page, the official page is the one that binds.

Highbar is a small, new launchpad. Nothing in this article is a reason to buy or sell a token, and no launchpad can guarantee demand.

Locked liquidity and a creator fee share are design choices. They do not make a token safe, and they do not stop sniper bots on their own.

Always confirm fee rates, graduation targets and liquidity rules on the live docs, or on the on-chain config, immediately before you sign.

A useful way to use a comparison like this is to write down the three rules you actually care about — the curve fee, the creator share, and what happens to liquidity at graduation — and check those three lines on each official page, rather than choosing a launchpad by name or by a social-media clip.

If you are launching, prepare the token name, ticker, image and any links first, then read the fee table for the exact config you are about to sign, including whether an anti-sniper or app fee applies. If you are trading, check the mint, the pool, and the live fee on the confirmation screen.
