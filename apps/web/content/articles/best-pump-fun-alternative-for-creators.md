"Best pump.fun alternative" means different things to different people. Traders want liquidity. Creators usually want something more specific: a fair share of the fees their coin generates, in both phases of its life. This guide compares creator payouts on pump.fun and five alternatives, using each platform's own pages. One of them is Highbar, which we run, so read that section as a disclosure too.

Checked on 12 November 2026. Platforms change fees often; follow the links before you launch.

## First, the market context

Reach still matters more than fee tables. [MemeFees](https://memefees.com/chains/solana), using DefiLlama data, showed these shares of Solana launchpad fees for the 30 days to 10 November 2026:

| Platform | 30-day fee share |
| --- | --- |
| Pump.fun | 80.8% |
| StonkFun | 6.5% |
| Raydium LaunchLab | 5.7% |
| Bags | 0.6% |

A bigger creator share on a quieter platform can pay less than a small share on a busy one. Keep that in mind through every table below.

## Platform by platform

### Pump.fun: the baseline

Pump.fun pays 0.30% on curve trades. After graduation to PumpSwap, the creator fee jumps to 0.95% in the 420–1,470 SOL market-cap band, then steps down tier by tier to 0.05% above 98,240 SOL. Creators can redirect the fee to holders (Holder Rewards) or a charity. Its huge audience is the reason it remains the default. More detail in our [pump.fun fees explainer](/articles/pump-fun-fees-explained).

### Bags: most flexible fee sharing

Bags runs on Meteora DBC and offers seven SOL fee modes. In Default mode, trades pay 2% before and after migration, split between the platform and a claimers pool, with 25% of post-migration fees compounded into liquidity. For new fee-share v2 configs, Bags' [deployer fees page](https://docs.bags.fm/how-to-guides/deployer-fees) says the wallet that pays to create the config gets 25% of the claimers pool. Bags is the pick if you want to split fees among several wallets or social accounts.

### Jupiter Studio: simple 50/50 for life

Jupiter Studio launches on Meteora DBC and graduates to DAMM v2. Its docs describe a 1% fee in meme mode, or 1% or 2% in custom mode, split 50% creator and 50% Jupiter for the token's whole life. Custom mode also allows creator vesting of up to 80% of supply.

### StonkFun: stock-paired coins

StonkFun pairs coins with tokenized stocks and many other assets on Raydium LaunchLab. Its launch form describes a 1% default pool fee split roughly 50/50, about 0.5% to the creator, with a 2% option. Raydium's public config data shows StonkFun's graduated LP is locked to the platform. Our [StonkFun launch guide](/articles/how-to-launch-on-stonkfun) walks through it.

### Moonshot: app-first launches

Moonshot's [creator fees page](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create/creator-fees) lists a 0.5% dynamic fee before a coin bonds and 0.3% after, with creators earning 0.25% pre-bond and up to 0.15% post-bond. Its [bonding page](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create/bonding.md) says bonding starts at a $1 million market cap. Payouts are hourly once your unpaid balance exceeds $1. Traders separately pay Moonshot's app trading fee, 2.5% (about $0.99 minimum) on trades of $2 to $100 and 1% above, per its [fees page](https://support.moonshot.com/trade/fees).

### Highbar: creator-chosen graduation bar

Highbar launches every token on Meteora DBC with a 1% curve fee. Meteora keeps 0.2% and the creator gets 0.4% on Standard (85 SOL target), 0.496% on Higher (150 SOL) or 0.6% on Max (250 SOL). On Higher, the creator also gets half of the 1% migration fee, 0.75 SOL. After graduation, the DAMM v2 pool charges 1%, and with the creator LP rebate creators get 0.6%, 0.65% or 0.7% of post-migration volume. Fees are claimed in SOL from Portfolio, and the rebate is sent automatically once it reaches 0.01 SOL.

What Highbar doesn't offer: fee sharing among multiple wallets, creator vesting, custom pairs or a big audience. It is new and small.

## How to choose as a creator

1. **Estimate realistic volume first.** A share of zero volume is zero.
2. **Look at both phases.** Some platforms pay more on the curve, others after graduation.
3. **Check who owns the graduated liquidity.** Locked to the platform, burned, or split with the creator.
4. **Check what traders pay.** App fees and high pool fees can discourage trading.
5. **Read the platform's own page**, not a summary like this one, before you sign.
