StonkFun made "stock-paired memecoins" a category on Solana: launch a coin that trades against a tokenized stock or almost any other token, and optionally pay holders rewards in that token. If you are looking for StonkFun alternatives, you might want a different pair asset, a different fee split or a launchpad with a different graduation model. This guide recaps StonkFun from its own pages and Raydium's config data, then compares the main alternatives. Highbar, one of them, is the launchpad we run.

Checked on 16 November 2026. These platforms change quickly, so read the live docs before launching.

## StonkFun in brief

From the [StonkFun launch form](https://www.stonkfun.xyz/launch) and [rewards page](https://www.stonkfun.xyz/rewards):

- **Pair (quote) token:** almost any token, grouped into categories such as xStocks, PreStocks, Tessera, Sunrise, currencies, baskets and SOL.
- **Pool fee:** 1% or 2%. On the 1% default, about 0.5% accrues to the creator and about 0.5% to the platform.
- **Reward coins:** newer reward launches are funded by a 1% or 3% transfer tax on every transfer and pay holders pro rata.
- **Platform revenue:** StonkFun's [revenue page](https://www.stonkfun.xyz/revenue) says about 60% is used to buy $STONK on the open market and burn it.
- **Infrastructure:** Raydium LaunchLab. Raydium's public [platform list](https://launch-mint-v1.raydium.io/main/platforms?page=1&pageSize=100) shows two StonkFun configs with a 1% platform fee rate, a 0 on-chain creator fee rate and the full graduated LP locked to the platform.

Our [StonkFun launch guide](/articles/how-to-launch-on-stonkfun) walks through the form step by step.

## Why look for a StonkFun alternative?

Reasons you might look elsewhere:

1. **You want an on-chain creator fee or a creator LP position** rather than a platform-forwarded share.
2. **You do not want a transfer tax** on your holders' sells and transfers.
3. **You want a different pair**, or just SOL.
4. **You want a different graduation venue**, such as Meteora DAMM v2.

### Float: the closest StonkFun alternative

Float also pairs coins with tokenized stocks and runs on Raydium LaunchLab. Per [Float's docs](https://www.floatlaunch.xyz/docs), rechecked on 23 November 2026, new standard coins pay a flat 1% fee. Builder launches can pick 1%, 2% or 2.5%, and standard coins launched earlier at 2% or 2.5% keep their rate. After Raydium's 0.25% cut on the curve, Float splits what it collects on standard coins: 17.5% to holders in the paired token, 30% to $FLOAT buybacks and burns, 15% to traders by score, 15% to $FLOAT holders and 22.5% to the treasury. The creator share on a standard coin is 0%. A builder launch sends 50% of what Float collects to a fee wallet. Coins graduate at about $150,000 market cap and the liquidity is locked with Raydium's Burn & Earn. Float discloses that its program has not had an outside audit yet. Our [Float explainer](/articles/float-launchpad-explained) covers its rules and risk notes.

**Pick Float** if you want stock pairs and holder rewards without a transfer tax, and accept that a standard coin pays the creator nothing.

### Bags: custom quotes on Meteora

Bags documents launching on a bonding curve with a custom quote, including supported stock tokens, in its [launch guides](https://docs.bags.fm/how-to-guides/launch-token). Its SOL fee modes are described on its [fee page](https://docs.bags.fm/how-to-guides/customize-token-fees), and fees can be shared across up to 100 earners. Our [Bags fees explainer](/articles/bags-fm-fees-explained) has the details.

**Pick Bags** if you want Meteora infrastructure, a non-SOL quote and fee sharing with collaborators.

### Bonk.fun: Raydium with the BONK community

Bonk.fun also runs on Raydium LaunchLab. Per Raydium's platform list, two letsbonk.fun configs carry a 1.25% platform fee rate and a 0 creator fee rate, with almost all graduated LP burned. [SOLTidy's comparison](https://soltidy.com/blog/solana-launchpads-compared) lists SOL and USD1 as its quote options.

**Pick Bonk.fun** for the community, not for creator fees.

### Jupiter Studio: USDC launches with vesting

Jupiter's [Studio docs](https://docs.jup.ag/user-docs/launch/studio) describe a 1% or 2% fee split 50/50 with the creator for the token's whole life, USDC, SOL or JUP as quote tokens in custom mode, optional anti-sniping and creator vesting.

**Pick Jupiter Studio** for USDC pairs, vesting and Jupiter's visibility.

### Highbar: SOL pairs with a creator-chosen bar

Highbar does not offer stock pairs. Its trade-off is simplicity. From the [Highbar docs](/docs):

- **Graduation targets:** 85, 150 or 250 SOL, chosen by the creator.
- **1% curve fee**, with 0.4%, 0.496% or 0.6% to the creator, and no extra app fee on the website.
- **After graduation**, liquidity is locked forever in a creator position and a platform position; creators receive 0.6%–0.7% of volume including the creator LP rebate.
- **No transfer tax**, no team allocation, fixed 1,000,000,000 supply and mint authority off.
- **Optional anti-sniper fee** from 25% decaying to about 1% over 60 seconds. It only reduces the early-buy advantage. It does not stop bots, bundles or rug pulls. Locked liquidity is not a guarantee of price or safety.

**Pick Highbar** if you want a SOL pair, an on-chain creator share on both sides of graduation and a locked creator LP position, and you accept a smaller audience.

## Risks that apply to every stock-paired launch

StonkFun and Float both point out that tokenized stocks are issued by third parties, not the companies, and a coin paired with one gives you no rights in the company. If an issuer pauses transfers, trading and rewards for paired coins can stop. Thin quote-token liquidity can also make prices and rewards volatile. None of these platforms can make a coin succeed.

## Bottom line

The closest StonkFun alternative is Float. Bags and Jupiter Studio offer non-SOL quotes on Meteora, Bonk.fun offers Raydium with a big community, and Highbar offers a simple SOL launch with a creator share before and after graduation.
