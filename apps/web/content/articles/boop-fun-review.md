Boop.fun is one of the more distinctive Solana launchpads. It talks like a meme, calls every token a "cult", and sends most post-graduation fees to token holders instead of creators. People search "boop.fun review" because that model is unusual and its docs are informal. This review lays out how Boop works from its own [documentation](https://docs.boop.fun/token-deployment-101), notes where the docs disagree with themselves, and compares it with Highbar, the launchpad we run.

Checked on 16 November 2026. Boop's docs say some reward programs are paused or changing, so check the live pages.

## Boop.fun in brief

| | Boop.fun |
| --- | --- |
| Launch cost | Free to start on the website |
| Launch methods | Website, or tag Boop's bot on X |
| Creator pre-buy | Up to 50% of supply at creation |
| Curve | Boop's own bonding curve |
| Graduation | About 400 SOL market cap; liquidity moves to a Meteora pool |
| Post-graduation fee | 2% |
| Main fee recipient | Token holders (or a delegated social account) |

## How launching on Boop.fun works

Boop's [token deployment guide](https://docs.boop.fun/token-deployment-101) offers two routes.

### Via the website

You connect a wallet and fill in a token name, ticker, image (PNG or JPEG, no larger than 1920×1920 px and under 5 MB, per the [FAQ](https://docs.boop.fun/faqs)), a "cult lore" description, optional Telegram, X and website links, and a trading-fee recipient. Then you review and launch. Login works with a Solana wallet or an X account through Privy.

### Via X

You can post a generated tweet that tags Boop's bot, which replies with the token links. To manage an X-launched token on the website later, you sign in with the same X account.

### How much can the creator buy?

The deployment guide says the creator can buy up to 50% of the supply at creation. The FAQ says you can pre-buy "all" of the 74.32% on the curve, which would trigger an immediate graduation. The two pages are not consistent, so check the live form before relying on either number.

## Boop.fun's bonding curve and graduation

Per Boop's [bonding curve page](https://docs.boop.fun/the-boop-bonding-curve), roughly 75% of supply sits on the curve. Its [graduation page](https://docs.boop.fun/graduation) says a token graduates at a market cap of about 400 SOL. At that point:

- About **80 SOL** moves with the remaining tokens into a Meteora liquidity pool.
- **6 SOL** goes to the Boop team for Meteora deployment and other expenses.

The curve page instead describes the trigger as about 86 SOL accumulated on the curve, which roughly matches 80 SOL migrated plus the 6 SOL fee.

### Supply split, and another inconsistency

The deployment guide says about 20% of supply is set aside for the Meteora pool, 0.5% goes to BOOP stakers at graduation and 4.5% is burned. The [rewards page](https://docs.boop.fun/rewards) says 5% of supply is airdropped to stakers at graduation. Both describe roughly 5% leaving the curve and pool; they disagree on how much is burned versus airdropped.

## Boop.fun fees and who gets them

From the deployment guide, a **2% fee** applies to post-graduation trades, split like this:

| Recipient | Share of the 2% fee |
| --- | --- |
| Token holders or fee delegates | 62.5% |
| BOOP stakers | 6.25% |
| BOOP buyback bank | 6.25% |
| Future rewards and incentives | 12.5% |
| Platform | 12.5% |

Before graduation, all fees go to the platform. The docs we checked do not state the pre-graduation fee rate, so look at the trade screen before you buy.

### Holder rewards

Per the rewards page, holder rewards are paid in SOL straight to holders' wallets in proportion to how much supply each holds, with no claim needed. That is the core of Boop's pitch: holders, not creators, earn the trading fees.

### Fee delegation

Creators can send the holder share to a particular account instead. Boop's [fee rewards page](https://docs.boop.fun/claiming-ur-fee-rewards) says recipients can be X, GitHub, Instagram or TikTok accounts. They log in on Boop's fee claim page, verify the account (Instagram and TikTok by posting a code in the bio), and claim. The FAQ suggests this for tokens that pay homage to a person or meme.

### Graduation rewards

The graduation page notes that graduation rewards are currently paused while a newer "Temple of Moon" programme runs, which unlocks a mix of BOOP and SOL rewards for cults as they grow. Reward programmes like this change often.

## What we like and what to watch

**Strengths**

- Free to start, with a launch-from-X option.
- Holder rewards and fee delegation are genuinely different ideas.
- Clear post-graduation fee split.

**Things to check**

- Inconsistent numbers across doc pages (pre-buy limit, staker allocation).
- Creators earn no trading fee by default.
- A 6 SOL graduation cost goes to the team.
- Reward programmes are paused or changing.

## Boop.fun vs Highbar

| | Boop.fun | Highbar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curve | Boop's own | Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve |
| Graduation | About 400 SOL market cap (~86 SOL on the curve) | 85, 150 or 250 SOL, creator picks |
| Graduates to | Meteora pool | Meteora DAMM v2, liquidity locked forever |
| Fee before graduation | All to platform (rate not stated) | 1%, creator gets 0.4%–0.6% |
| Fee after graduation | 2%, 62.5% to holders | 1%, creator gets 0.6%–0.7% incl. LP rebate |
| Creator pre-buy | Up to 50% | Dev buy up to 20 SOL |
| Anti-sniper | Not described | Optional, 25% decaying to about 1% over 60 s |

Highbar numbers come from the [Highbar docs](/docs). The big philosophical difference: Boop pays holders, Highbar pays creators. Highbar's anti-sniper fee is optional and only makes the earliest buys more expensive. It does not stop bots or bundles. Read our [anti-sniper explainer](/articles/anti-sniper-fee-solana-token-launch) for the details, and our [Meteora DAMM v2 guide](/articles/meteora-damm-v2-explained-token-graduation) for what locked liquidity means.

## Verdict

Boop.fun suits creators who want their community to earn the fees, or who want to route fees to an artist or personality. It is less suited to creators who want to earn from trading themselves, and its docs need careful reading. If you would rather pick a graduation bar and keep a creator share on both sides of graduation, compare on the Highbar [launch page](/launch), or trade from your timeline with the [Highbar Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oioebamfkpahdnjkbbmldepkackabhed). On any platform, rewards depend on trading that may never happen.
