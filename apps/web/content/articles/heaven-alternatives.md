Heaven is a Solana AMM and launchpad with a published design that is easy to mis-copy: there is no migrating bonding curve, launches are free and take under five seconds, and creator fees depend on a category that can be reviewed. If you searched for a Heaven alternative, the useful test is whether the other product actually does one of those jobs. This guide uses Heaven's official [about](https://docs.heaven.xyz/about), [curve](https://docs.heaven.xyz/curve), [fees](https://docs.heaven.xyz/fees) and [permissioned](https://docs.heaven.xyz/permissioned) pages, checked on 16 November 2026, plus the official pages for the other products named here. None of them is affiliated with Heaven or with Highbar. Our longer read is [Heaven launchpad review](/articles/heaven-launchpad-review).

## What you would be leaving

Official Heaven curve docs say each pool moves as though it were seeded with 35 SOL of virtual SOL, that nobody can sell into that virtual SOL, and that trading starts on Heaven's AMM from day one. A $100k virtual migration cap exists so display tools can show a familiar "migration" event, but the pair does not change venue. Official fee docs list a 1% protocol fee below $100k market cap, then 0.25% on community coins and 0.5% on creator coins, plus a 0.1% or 1% creator fee by category. Official permissioned-fee docs say a self-tagged Creator launch is reviewed after $100k in volume, and that the tag is not a safety approval. Official permissionless docs say launches are free and take under five seconds.

If that combination — own AMM, virtual SOL, category review — is the job, most alternatives will fail the test on at least one line.

## Highbar, if you wanted a migrating Meteora curve and locked real LP

Highbar launches on a Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve and graduates at 85, 150 or 250 SOL into a DAMM v2 pool whose liquidity is locked forever in two positions. The curve fee is 1%, with a tiered creator share of 0.4% to 0.6% on the curve and 0.6% to 0.7% after graduation with the rebate. There is no virtual-SOL floor, no $100k display cap, and no Creator review. Highbar is small and new. It is a fit only if the job was a SOL-paired Meteora launch with a published tier table, not if the job was Heaven's AMM.

Use the [Highbar docs](/docs) and [launch page](/launch) only after that distinction is clear. The [Highbar Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oioebamfkpahdnjkbbmldepkackabhed) is a Highbar tool, not a Heaven setting.

## Jupiter Studio and other migrating-curve venues

Jupiter Studio also uses a bonding curve that graduates into a Meteora DAMM v2 pool. Meme mode graduates at a 75,000 USDC market cap with about 15,390 USDC raised and a 1% fee split 50/50 for life. Current Studio configs lock all graduated LP; some older configs let 50% unlock after a year. Studio has no Heaven-style category review. It is a closer cousin to Highbar than to Heaven, because both Studio and Highbar migrate.

Pump.fun remains the default search comparison: its own curve, a 1.25% curve fee, graduation to PumpSwap, and an official-app Platform Fee. It is not an AMM with virtual SOL. Raydium LaunchLab is the other large non-Meteora path, with official JustSendit and LaunchLab modes and a usual 0.25% CPMM fee after graduation. For a wider list, see [pump.fun alternatives](/articles/pump-fun-alternatives-compare-solana-launchpads) and [Meteora launchpad platforms compared](/articles/meteora-launchpad-platforms-compared).

## A clean way to choose

Write down the Heaven feature you actually wanted: a single AMM that never migrates, a virtual SOL floor, a reviewed creator fee, or a free sub-five-second deploy. Then open one official page for each alternative and check only those lines. A decaying early fee — Heaven's official Pro Pools table lists a 6-second linear decay on standard pools; Highbar offers an optional 60-second fee — is not a reason to treat either venue as safe. Locked real LP and a virtual floor are also different tools.

If the job was Heaven's AMM, stay on Heaven's official docs. If the job was a SOL-tier Meteora launch with locked DAMM v2 liquidity, Highbar is in the running, and only then should you read its launch form.

Locked liquidity and a creator fee share are design choices. They do not make a token safe, and they do not stop sniper bots on their own.

Always confirm fee rates, graduation targets and liquidity rules on the live docs, or on the on-chain config, immediately before you sign.

A useful way to use a comparison like this is to write down the three rules you actually care about — the curve fee, the creator share, and what happens to liquidity at graduation — and check those three lines on each official page, rather than choosing a launchpad by name or by a social-media clip.

If you are launching, prepare the token name, ticker, image and any links first, then read the fee table for the exact config you are about to sign, including whether an anti-sniper or app fee applies. If you are trading, check the mint, the pool, and the live fee on the confirmation screen.

Take your time, read the confirmation screen, and only sign if the live fee and destination pool match what you expected to pay.
