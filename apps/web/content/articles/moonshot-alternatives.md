Moonshot is best known as a simple mobile app for buying Solana tokens with a card, and its Create tab lets anyone launch a coin from the same app. If you are searching for Moonshot alternatives, you probably want either a different fee structure, an earlier graduation point or a bigger launch audience. This guide summarises how Moonshot Create works from its own [support docs](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create), then compares the main alternatives using each platform's published numbers. Highbar, one of the options, is the launchpad we run.

Checked on 16 November 2026. Note that Moonit, the former DEX Screener Moonshot launchpad, is a different product, per [MemeFees](https://memefees.com/launchpads/moonshot).

## How Moonshot Create works

Moonshot's [Create guide](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create) lists four required fields (ticker, name, image and description), optional website and X links, up to four extra photos or videos, and two numbers: your initial buy and your creator fee percentage. Then you swipe to create.

### Moonshot fees

Moonshot's [trading fees page](https://support.moonshot.com/trade/fees) uses tiers by trade size:

| Trade size | Moonshot fee |
| --- | --- |
| $2 to $100 | 2.5%, with a minimum of about $0.99 |
| Over $100 | 1% |

A separate "crypto fee" covers network and routing costs, and MoonPay charges its own fee on deposits and withdrawals.

For Create coins, the [creator fees page](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create/creator-fees) lists a dynamic fee of 0.5% before the coin bonds and 0.3% after. The creator earns up to half: 0.25% before bonding and up to 0.15% after, configurable in the create form. Payouts run hourly once the unpaid balance passes $1.

### Moonshot graduation

Per Moonshot's [bonding page](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create/bonding), bonding starts once a Create coin reaches a $1 million market cap, when it moves from a virtual bonding curve to a full liquidity pool. The page points to Meteora's DBC documentation for the mechanics, and bonded coins get a "Created on Moonshot" badge.

### Why people look for alternatives

Three practical reasons come up:

1. **The $1M bonding point is high.** Many curve launchpads graduate at around 85 SOL raised, far earlier.
2. **Small trades are expensive in the app.** A $20 buy pays the roughly $0.99 minimum, about 5%.
3. **Activity is low right now.** MemeFees, using DefiLlama, recorded about $59 in Moonshot fees on 14 November 2026 and about $10.8K over 30 days.

None of that makes Moonshot a bad product. Its card on-ramp and simple app are real strengths for newcomers.

## Moonshot alternatives compared

| Platform | Curve / model | Curve fee | Creator on curve | Graduation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Moonshot Create | Meteora DBC | 0.5% dynamic fee (plus app fee) | 0.25% | $1M market cap |
| Pump.fun | Own curve → PumpSwap | 1.25% | 0.30% | Fixed threshold |
| Jupiter Studio | Meteora DBC → DAMM v2 | 1% or 2% | 50% of the fee | Mode-dependent |
| Bags | Meteora DBC → DAMM v2 | 2% (Default mode) | 1% claimers pool | 85 SOL (Default) |
| Heaven | Own AMM, no curve | 1% below $100k | 0.1% or 1% after review | No migration |
| Highbar | Meteora DBC → DAMM v2 | 1% | 0.4%–0.6% by tier | 85, 150 or 250 SOL |

Sources are linked in each section below.

### Pump.fun: the biggest audience

Pump.fun's [fees page](https://pump.fun/docs/fees) lists 1.25% on curve trades (0.30% creator, 0.95% protocol), plus a 0.45% Platform Fee in its own web and mobile apps since 17 October 2026. MemeFees' [launchpad table](https://memefees.com/launchpads) put it first on Solana with about $1.05M in fees on 15 November 2026. If reach matters most, pump.fun is the obvious alternative. Our [pump.fun fees explainer](/articles/pump-fun-fees-explained) has the full schedule.

### Jupiter Studio: Meteora with vesting

Jupiter's [Studio docs](https://docs.jup.ag/user-docs/launch/studio) describe a 1% or 2% fee split 50/50 between creator and Jupiter for the token's whole life, optional anti-sniping in custom mode, creator vesting of up to 80% of supply and permanently locked LP after graduation. Good if you want vesting and Jupiter's visibility.

### Bags: flexible fee sharing

Bags' [fee guide](https://docs.bags.fm/how-to-guides/customize-token-fees) lists seven fee modes. Default charges 2% on both sides of graduation and graduates at 85 SOL, with fees shareable across several claimers, including social accounts. Good if you want to share fees with collaborators.

### Heaven: no bonding curve

Heaven's [fees page](https://docs.heaven.xyz/fees) lists free launches and a 1% protocol fee below $100k market cap. Tokens trade on Heaven's own AMM from the first block with "virtual SOL" depth, and creator fees above a 0.1% base depend on Heaven's review. Our [Heaven review](/articles/heaven-launchpad-review) covers the model.

### Highbar: pick your graduation bar

Highbar launches every token on Meteora DBC and graduates it to a Meteora DAMM v2 pool with liquidity locked forever. From the [Highbar docs](/docs):

- **Three targets.** Standard graduates at 85 SOL, Higher at 150 SOL, Max at 250 SOL.
- **1% curve fee** with no extra app fee on the website. The creator gets 0.4%, 0.496% or 0.6% by tier.
- **After graduation**, creators receive 0.6% to 0.7% of volume including the creator LP rebate.
- **Optional anti-sniper fee** from 25% decaying to about 1% over 60 seconds. It only reduces the early sniping advantage; it does not stop bots or bundles.

Compared with Moonshot, the main differences are a much earlier graduation point and a larger creator share before graduation. Highbar has no card on-ramp and a much smaller audience. You can trade Highbar tokens from X with the [Highbar Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oioebamfkpahdnjkbbmldepkackabhed).

## How to choose

- **Stay on Moonshot** if your audience buys with cards in a mobile app and you are happy with a high bonding point.
- **Go to pump.fun** if you want the most eyes on your launch.
- **Try Jupiter Studio or Bags** if you want vesting or fee sharing on Meteora.
- **Try Heaven** if you prefer no curve and no migration.
- **Try Highbar** if you want to choose an 85, 150 or 250 SOL target and earn a creator fee on both sides of graduation.

Whichever you pick, read the live fee pages before you launch and size any dev buy as money you can lose. The [Highbar launch page](/launch) previews each tier without signing.
