Moonshot's Create product is easy to search and easy to mix up with other "moonshot" names. This review uses only Moonshot's official support pages for [creating a coin](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create), [bonding](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create/bonding), [creator fees](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create/creator-fees) and [trade fees](https://support.moonshot.com/trade/fees), checked on 16 November 2026, and compares those published rules with the [Highbar docs](/docs). Moonshot is not affiliated with Highbar.

## How a Create launch works

Official Create docs describe a short form. Required fields are ticker, name, image and description. Optional fields include a website URL, an X username, and up to four additional photos or videos, with a one-video limit of 30 seconds. You then set an initial buy amount and the percentage of creator fees you want when the coin bonds, and swipe to create.

That is an app-style flow, not a developer SDK launch. This article does not invent button labels beyond that official stepper.

## Bonding at a $1 million market cap

Moonshot's bonding page says bonding is the automatic migration from a virtual bonding-curve pool to a complete liquidity pool, and that the process begins once a Create coin reaches a $1 million market cap. The same page points readers to [Meteora's DBC overview](https://docs.meteora.ag/overview/products/dbc/what-is-dbc) for a detailed look at that kind of curve. After a coin created on Moonshot bonds, official docs say it receives a purple "Created on Moonshot" badge and has a higher chance of being verified and shown on Moonshot's home page. That is a visibility rule, not a safety review, and it is not a reason to buy the coin.

A $1 million threshold is a different job from Highbar's 85, 150 or 250 SOL targets, or from many Solana launchpads that graduate at tens or hundreds of SOL. If you wanted a short SOL target, Moonshot Create is not a substitute.

## Fees, in two layers

Creators and app traders are charged under two official tables.

On the coin itself, [creator-fee docs](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create/creator-fees) list:

| Stage | Dynamic fee on each transaction | Creator's share (up to 50%) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before bonding | 0.5% | 0.25% of the trade |
| After bonding | 0.3% | Up to 0.15% of the trade, set in the create form |

Payouts are processed hourly if the unpaid balance exceeds $1. Those shares are mechanics. They are not a forecast of income.

Separately, trades placed in the Moonshot app pay Moonshot's own fee plus a "crypto fee" that official docs describe as covering network processing and routing, plus price impact, which is not a fee. The published app tiers are 2.5% with a roughly $0.99 minimum on trades of $2 to $100, and 1% on trades of $100 or more. Default slippage tolerance is dynamic, up to 5%, and can be changed in settings. Deposits and withdrawals may also incur a MoonPay fee; official docs point to MoonPay's own fee page rather than restating it. Moonshot says it does not take an administrative fee for sends, though the network fee still applies.

The app fee is easy to miss if you only read the 0.5% / 0.3% creator-fee table. Traders should read the confirmation screen. Our [creator fees guide](/articles/memecoin-creator-fees-solana-launchpad) is about launchpad creator shares in general; it is not a Moonshot payout calculator.

## How Highbar compares

Highbar is a website launchpad. You open [Launch](/launch), connect a wallet, add a name, ticker, image and optional links, pick a tier, and sign. Graduation is 85, 150 or 250 SOL. The curve fee is 1%, with the creator earning 0.4% to 0.6% of each curve trade and 0.6% to 0.7% after graduation with the LP rebate. Liquidity is locked forever in two Meteora DAMM v2 positions. There is no extra website app fee; the [Highbar Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oioebamfkpahdnjkbbmldepkackabhed) adds 0.02% of the SOL amount on extension trades. An optional anti-sniper fee starts at 25% and decays to about 1% over 60 seconds. It does not stop bots. Official Moonshot pages cited here do not describe an equivalent decaying launch fee, and this review does not invent one.

| | Moonshot Create (official support pages) | Highbar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch surface | In-app Create flow, swipe to create | Website form |
| Bonding / graduation | $1 million market cap | 85, 150 or 250 SOL |
| Coin trading fee | 0.5% pre-bond, 0.3% post-bond | 1% on the curve and 1% in DAMM v2 |
| Creator share | Up to 0.25% pre-bond, up to 0.15% post-bond | 0.4%–0.6% on the curve, 0.6%–0.7% after |
| App or extension fee | 2.5% or 1% in the Moonshot app, by trade size | No extra website fee; extension 0.02% |

Pick Moonshot if you wanted that app flow, the $1 million bonding rule, and hourly creator payouts above $1. Pick Highbar if you wanted a SOL-paired Meteora launch with a published tier table and locked DAMM v2 liquidity, and you accept that Highbar is small. For a wider set of venues, see [pump.fun alternatives](/articles/pump-fun-alternatives-compare-solana-launchpads). Read both official pages before you sign, and use Highbar's [docs](/docs) only if Highbar is the product you actually want.

Figures in this article were checked on 16 November 2026 against the official pages linked above.

Those pages change, so re-read them before you launch or trade, and do not treat this guide as a substitute for the live docs.

If a figure here disagrees with an official page, the official page is the one that binds.

Highbar is a small, new launchpad. Nothing in this article is a reason to buy or sell a token, and no launchpad can guarantee demand.

Locked liquidity and a creator fee share are design choices. They do not make a token safe, and they do not stop sniper bots on their own.

Always confirm fee rates, graduation targets and liquidity rules on the live docs, or on the on-chain config, immediately before you sign.

A useful way to use a comparison like this is to write down the three rules you actually care about — the curve fee, the creator share, and what happens to liquidity at graduation — and check those three lines on each official page, rather than choosing a launchpad by name or by a social-media clip.
