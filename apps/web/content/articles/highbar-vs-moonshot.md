Highbar and Moonshot Create show up in the same alternative searches and do not do the same job. This comparison uses the [Highbar docs](/docs) and Moonshot's official [Create](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create), [bonding](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create/bonding), [creator fees](https://support.moonshot.com/coins/create/creator-fees) and [trade fees](https://support.moonshot.com/trade/fees) pages, checked on 16 November 2026. Highbar is our product. Where Moonshot's official rules are a better fit, this article says so.

## At a glance

| | Moonshot Create | Highbar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surface | In-app Create flow | Website at [Launch](/launch) |
| Bonding or graduation | $1 million market cap | 85, 150 or 250 SOL |
| Coin trading fee | 0.5% before bonding, 0.3% after | 1% on the curve, 1% in DAMM v2 |
| Creator share | Up to 0.25% pre-bond, up to 0.15% post-bond | 0.4%–0.6% on the curve, 0.6%–0.7% after, with rebate |
| Trader app or extension fee | 2.5% or 1% in the official app, by size | No extra website fee; extension 0.02% |
| Liquidity after the threshold | Migrates to a complete liquidity pool; official pages cited here do not state a lock rule | Locked forever in two DAMM v2 positions |

## The threshold is the first decision

If you want a coin to stay on a curve until it reaches a $1 million market cap, Moonshot documents that rule and Highbar does not. Highbar's three SOL targets are much earlier events in SOL terms, and they move raised SOL plus 20% of supply into a locked Meteora DAMM v2 pool. Moonshot's bonding page describes a move from a virtual bonding-curve pool to a complete liquidity pool and links to Meteora's DBC overview for background. This article does not invent a Moonshot lock percentage, because the official pages cited here do not state one.

## Fees, including the app layer

Moonshot's creator share is smaller on paper: up to 0.25% of each pre-bond trade and up to 0.15% after bonding, paid hourly above a $1 unpaid balance. Highbar's creator share is 0.4% to 0.6% on the curve and 0.6% to 0.7% after graduation with the rebate. Traders on Highbar pay the 1% curve or pool fee, plus 0.02% only if they use the [Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oioebamfkpahdnjkbbmldepkackabhed). Traders in the official Moonshot app also face the 2.5% or 1% app tiers, plus network and routing costs, which is a different all-in experience. See [memecoin creator fees](/articles/memecoin-creator-fees-solana-launchpad) for how to read a share as a share, not as income.

Highbar's optional anti-sniper fee starts at 25% and decays to about 1% over 60 seconds. Official Moonshot pages cited here do not describe a matching decaying launch fee, so none is attributed.

## Who each product suits

Moonshot suits people who want the official Create flow, swipe-to-launch, hourly payouts, and a $1 million bonding badge inside that app. Highbar suits people who want a SOL-paired Meteora launch, a chosen 85, 150 or 250 SOL target, a published creator share on both phases, and locked DAMM v2 liquidity, and who accept a small, new venue. They overlap only at the shallow level of "a place to launch a coin."
