There is no single best Solana launchpad. There is a best launchpad **for a specific goal**: maximum audience, the highest creator share, stock-paired coins, a quick meme, or a token with a longer runway. This guide compares six platforms people are searching for this month, using each platform's own documentation for the numbers, and tells you plainly where Highbar fits. We run Highbar, so treat our section as a disclosure as well as a description.

Snapshot date: 11 November 2026. Fees and thresholds change, so check each linked source before you act.

## Platform by platform

### Pump.fun: the audience leader

Pump.fun's [fees page](https://pump.fun/docs/fees) lists free coin creation, a 1.25% curve fee (0.30% to the creator, 0.95% protocol), a flat 0.015 SOL graduation fee and PumpSwap fees that fall from 1.25% to 0.30% as market cap grows. Since 17 October 2026, trades in pump.fun's own web and mobile apps add a 0.45% Platform Fee (0.40% with a referral code). **Best for:** reach. Nothing else has as many eyes on new coins.

### Bonk.fun (LetsBonk): LaunchLab with BONK branding

Bonk.fun runs on Raydium LaunchLab. Raydium's public platform list shows two letsbonk.fun configs with a platform fee rate of 1.25%, a creator fee rate of 0 and nearly all graduated LP burned (a third config charges 1%). **Best for:** traders and creators who want the BONK community and Raydium liquidity, and are fine without a curve creator fee.

### Bags: the most fee options

Bags documents seven SOL fee modes, from a 0.25%/1% split to a 10% "Paper Hand Tax" mode. Default mode is 2% before and after migration, half to the platform and half to the fee claimers, with 25% of post-migration fees compounded into liquidity. Most modes graduate at 85 SOL into Meteora DAMM v2. **Best for:** creators who want to share fees with other X accounts or wallets.

### Jupiter Studio: configurable and Jupiter-native

Jupiter Studio's [user docs](https://docs.jup.ag/user-docs/launch/studio) describe Meme and Custom modes, a 1% or 2% fee split 50/50 between creator and Jupiter for the token's whole life, optional anti-sniping from 1 to 5 minutes, up to 80% creator vesting in Custom mode, and permanently locked LP after graduation to Meteora DAMM v2. **Best for:** creators who want vesting options and Jupiter's visibility.

### Float: this week's newcomer

Float pairs every coin with a token such as a tokenized stock, SOL or USDC. Its [docs](https://www.floatlaunch.xyz/docs), rechecked on 23 November 2026, list a $20,000 starting market cap and graduation at about $150,000 into a Raydium pool locked with Burn & Earn. New standard coins pay a flat 1% fee. Builder launches can pick 1%, 2% or 2.5%, and older standard coins keep a 2% or 2.5% rate. On standard coins Float splits what it collects 17.5% to holders, 30% to a $FLOAT buyback, 15% to traders, 15% to $FLOAT holders and 22.5% to the treasury, with a 0% creator share. The docs also say the Float program has not had an outside audit yet. **Best for:** people who specifically want stock-paired coins.

### Highbar: pick your own bar

Highbar launches every token on Meteora DBC and graduates it to a Meteora DAMM v2 pool with permanently locked liquidity. Per the [Highbar docs](/docs):

- **Three graduation targets:** Standard 85 SOL, Higher 150 SOL, Max 250 SOL.
- **1% curve fee** with no extra app fee on the website. Creator share on the curve: 0.4%, 0.496% or 0.6% by tier.
- **Optional anti-sniper fee**, off by default: 25% at launch, decaying to about 1% over 60 seconds. It only makes early buys more expensive. It does not stop bots, bundles or rug pulls. Locked liquidity is not a guarantee of price or safety. See [how the decaying fee works](/articles/anti-sniper-fee-solana-token-launch).
- **Dev Lock** with Jupiter Lock for creators who want to lock their own tokens publicly.
- **Fixed token facts:** 1,000,000,000 supply, mint authority off, immutable metadata, no team allocation or presale.

**What Highbar isn't:** big. It is new, with a small token list and far less traffic than pump.fun. **Best for:** creators who want to choose how much SOL their token must raise before graduation, and who value locked liquidity and public lock data.

## How to choose

Answer these honestly:

1. **Do you need the largest audience?** Then pump.fun is hard to beat.
2. **Do you want a creator fee, and on which phase?** Bonk.fun's curve creator fee is 0; Jupiter Studio pays 50% of fees for the token's life; Highbar and Bags pay creators on both phases.
3. **Do you care where liquidity goes?** Read each platform's graduation rules, and confirm on-chain.
4. **Do you want anti-sniper or vesting tools?** Jupiter Studio and Highbar document optional anti-sniper fees; Jupiter Studio and Raydium LaunchLab support vesting.
5. **Can you verify the numbers?** Prefer platforms that publish fee pages, configs and addresses.

Before buying any token, whatever the platform, run through our [checklist for checking a Solana token](/articles/how-to-check-a-solana-token-before-buying).
