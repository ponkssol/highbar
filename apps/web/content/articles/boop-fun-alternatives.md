Boop.fun is a Solana launchpad with a distinctive official reward design: after graduation, most of a 2% fee is shared with token holders or with a delegated X account. If you are looking for a Boop.fun alternative, start by deciding whether that reward design is the job you wanted. This guide uses Boop's official [token deployment](https://docs.boop.fun/token-deployment-101), [bonding curve](https://docs.boop.fun/the-boop-bonding-curve), [graduation](https://docs.boop.fun/graduation) and [rewards](https://docs.boop.fun/rewards) pages, checked on 16 November 2026, plus the official pages for the other products named here. None of them is affiliated with Highbar.

## What official Boop pages actually say

Launches can start for free on the [Boop create page](https://boop.fun/create/token) or by tagging [@beeponboop](https://x.com/beeponboop) on X. Official deployment docs put roughly 75% of supply on the bonding curve, set about 20% aside for a Meteora pool, and describe 0.5% going to BOOP stakers at graduation with an additional 4.5% burned. The rewards page, on the other hand, says 5% of supply is airdropped to BOOP stakers at graduation. Both describe roughly 5% leaving the circulating launch path; they do not use the same words, so check the live pages.

Graduation figures also differ across official pages, and this article does not reconcile them. The bonding-curve page says the curve accumulates about 86 SOL, making up about 75% of supply, before a Meteora pool is created with the final 20% (with 5% already set aside for stakers). The graduation page says the token needs a market cap of about 400 SOL, then 80 SOL plus remaining tokens move to the pool and 6 SOL goes to the team, and it adds that the curve needs about 80 SOL and 206,900,000 tokens, "not exactly 400 SOL." If you trade or launch on Boop, use the live docs, not a single remembered number.

After graduation, official deployment and rewards pages describe a **2%** fee on trades, with 62.5% to holders or fee delegates, 6.25% to BOOP stakers, 6.25% to a buyback bank, 12.5% to future rewards, and 12.5% to the platform. Before graduation, official docs say all fees go to the platform. Graduation rewards were marked as paused on the graduation page when this was checked, as the Temple of Moon takes over. Holder rewards after graduation, official rewards docs say, go directly to holders' wallets with no claim required, unless fees were delegated.

If holder rewards or fee delegation is why you looked at Boop, most "alternatives" will not copy that design.

## Highbar, if you wanted a simpler Meteora path

Highbar also uses a Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve and graduates into a locked Meteora DAMM v2 pool. That is the overlap. Highbar does not pay 62.5% of a 2% fee to holders, does not delegate fees to an X account, and does not airdrop supply to a platform token's stakers.

| | Boop.fun (official pages, with the disagreements noted above) | Highbar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch | Free website form, or via X | Website form at [Launch](/launch) |
| Graduation | Official pages give both ~86 SOL on the curve and ~400 SOL market cap | 85, 150 or 250 SOL, chosen at launch |
| Post-graduation fee | 2%, 62.5% to holders or delegates | 1% DAMM v2 fee, creator 0.6%–0.7% with rebate |
| Liquidity | Moves to a Meteora pool; official pages cited here do not state a permanent lock | Locked forever in two DAMM v2 positions |
| Anti-sniper | Not described on the official pages cited here | Optional 25% to about 1% over 60 seconds |

Highbar suits creators who want a published SOL tier, a creator share on both phases, and locked DAMM v2 liquidity, and who accept a small venue. It is not a holder-reward clone of Boop.

## Other Meteora options, and non-Meteora ones

Jupiter Studio also graduates into a Meteora DAMM v2 pool, with a 1% or 2% fee split 50/50 for life, optional vesting, and, in current configs, permanently locked LP. It does not document Boop-style holder rewards. Bags documents seven fee modes and a claimers pool, which is a different kind of sharing. See [Meteora launchpad platforms compared](/articles/meteora-launchpad-platforms-compared) and [pump.fun alternatives](/articles/pump-fun-alternatives-compare-solana-launchpads) for the wider map.

Pump.fun remains the default search comparison: a 1.25% curve fee, graduation to PumpSwap, and an official-app Platform Fee. It is not a Meteora holder-reward venue.

Write down the Boop feature you actually wanted — holder rewards, an X launch, a free create flow, or a Meteora pool — and open one official page for each alternative. Locked liquidity and holder rewards are design choices. They do not make a token safe. If the job was a SOL-paired Highbar launch, read the [docs](/docs) first. The [Highbar Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oioebamfkpahdnjkbbmldepkackabhed) is only relevant after that choice, not as an argument for leaving Boop.

Figures in this article were checked on 16 November 2026 against the official pages linked above.

Those pages change, so re-read them before you launch or trade, and do not treat this guide as a substitute for the live docs.

If a figure here disagrees with an official page, the official page is the one that binds.

Highbar is a small, new launchpad. Nothing in this article is a reason to buy or sell a token, and no launchpad can guarantee demand.

Locked liquidity and a creator fee share are design choices. They do not make a token safe, and they do not stop sniper bots on their own.

Always confirm fee rates, graduation targets and liquidity rules on the live docs, or on the on-chain config, immediately before you sign.

A useful way to use a comparison like this is to write down the three rules you actually care about — the curve fee, the creator share, and what happens to liquidity at graduation — and check those three lines on each official page, rather than choosing a launchpad by name or by a social-media clip.

If you are launching, prepare the token name, ticker, image and any links first, then read the fee table for the exact config you are about to sign, including whether an anti-sniper or app fee applies. If you are trading, check the mint, the pool, and the live fee on the confirmation screen.
