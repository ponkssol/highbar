Boop.fun and pump.fun both start tokens on a bonding curve, but they reward different people. Pump.fun pays the creator a slice of every trade. Boop's documented model sends most of its post-graduation fee to the token's holders, and lets creators launch from X by tagging a bot. This comparison uses Boop's [token deployment](https://docs.boop.fun/token-deployment-101), [graduation](https://docs.boop.fun/graduation), [rewards](https://docs.boop.fun/rewards) and [FAQ](https://docs.boop.fun/faqs) pages, and pump.fun's [fees](https://pump.fun/docs/fees) and [bonding curve](https://pump.fun/docs/bonding-curve) pages, checked on 17 November 2026.

We run Highbar, a third option covered briefly at the end. Neither Boop nor pump.fun is affiliated with Highbar.

## Boop.fun vs pump.fun at a glance

| | Boop.fun | Pump.fun |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch cost | Free via the website | No platform fee; network fee and account deposit |
| Launch from X | Yes, by tagging @beeponboop | Not described in the fee or curve docs |
| Fee before graduation | All fees go to the platform (rate not stated) | 1.25% (0.30% creator, 0.95% protocol) |
| Fee after graduation | 2%, with 62.5% to holders or a delegate | Tiered PumpSwap fee, 1.25% down to 0.30% |
| Creator income | Via fee delegation, or as a holder | 0.30% on the curve, tiered creator fee after |
| Creator pre-buy | Up to 50% at creation (FAQ: all 74.32% triggers auto-graduation) | Not limited on the pages we read |
| Graduation | About 400 SOL market cap (80 SOL to the pool, 6 SOL to the team) | Market-cap threshold, 0.015 SOL fee |
| Pool after graduation | A dedicated Meteora pool | Protocol-owned canonical PumpSwap pool |
| Extra app fee | Not described | 0.45% in the pump.fun apps (0.40% with referral) |

## How launching works

Boop gives two routes. On the website you connect a wallet and enter a name, ticker, image (PNG or JPEG, up to 1920×1920 px and 5 MB), "cult lore", fee recipient and optional links, then review and launch. Or you post a tweet that tags beep, Boop's X bot, which replies with confirmation and links. You can log in with a Solana wallet or an X account through Privy.

Pump.fun's flow is the familiar one. You create a coin with no platform fee, paired with SOL, USDC or a supported Custom Pair asset, and it trades on the curve from the first second.

## Supply and the curve

Boop's deployment page puts about 75% of supply on the bonding curve, about 20% aside for the Meteora pool, 0.5% for BOOP stakers at graduation, and 4.5% burned. Its rewards page instead says 5% of supply is airdropped to stakers at graduation. Both describe about 5% leaving the curve path. They are worded differently, and we do not pick one. The creator can buy up to 50% of supply at creation, per the deployment page. The FAQ says a creator can pre-buy all 74.32% on the curve, which triggers an automatic graduation.

Pump.fun describes its curve as a constant-product AMM on virtual reserves. The coin graduates when its market cap hits a threshold, and the whole pool migrates atomically to PumpSwap. The curve page does not print the threshold as a number.

## Fees compared

### Before graduation

Pump.fun charges 1.25% per curve trade: 0.30% to the creator, 0.95% to the protocol and none to LP. Since 17 October 2026, trades in the pump.fun web or mobile app add a 0.45% Platform Fee, or 0.40% with a referral code.

Boop's deployment page says all fees go to the platform before graduation. It does not give a percentage, so we do not invent one. Check the trade confirmation if you are buying a Boop token still on its curve.

### After graduation

Boop's documented post-graduation fee is 2%, split like this:

| Recipient | Share of the 2% |
| --- | --- |
| Token holders, or the fee delegate | 62.5% |
| BOOP stakers | 6.25% |
| BOOP buyback bank | 6.25% |
| Future rewards and incentives | 12.5% |
| Platform | 12.5% |

Holder rewards go straight to holders' wallets, with no claim needed, in proportion to how much supply each holds. Boop says it means the SOL portion when it talks about trading fees.

Pump.fun's canonical PumpSwap pools charge a tiered fee by market cap. For SOL-paired coins it is 1.25% below 420 SOL and falls to 0.30% at 98,240 SOL and above. The creator fee within it also changes by tier. Our [pump.fun fees explainer](/articles/pump-fun-fees-explained) has the tables.

## Who gets paid

This is the real choice. On pump.fun, the creator earns 0.30% of each curve trade by default. At launch they can redirect it to holders through Holder Rewards or opt in to a charity.

On Boop, the default route sends 62.5% of the post-graduation fee to holders. A creator can delegate that portion to an X account instead, which Boop suggests for tokens that pay homage to a person or meme. The claim page covers claiming delegated rewards after linking an account. A Boop creator who wants income either delegates fees or holds the token.

## Graduation details

Boop's graduation page targets a market cap of about 400 SOL. It notes this is not exact: the curve needs about 80 SOL and 206,900,000 tokens. At graduation, 80 SOL moves to a Meteora pool with the remaining tokens and 6 SOL goes to the team for deployment and other costs. The separate bonding-curve page mentions about 86 SOL. On the day we checked, graduation rewards were paused while Boop's Temple of Moon program takes over.

Pump.fun charges 0.015 SOL per graduation. It says the migrated liquidity becomes a protocol-owned PumpSwap pool that pump.fun does not seed or remove.

## Where Highbar fits

Highbar pays the creator directly, like pump.fun, but runs on Meteora like Boop's graduated pools. Per the [Highbar docs](/docs):

- Curve trades pay 1%. Meteora keeps 0.2% and the creator earns 0.4%, 0.496% or 0.6% depending on the tier.
- The creator picks a graduation target of 85, 150 or 250 SOL. Liquidity then moves to a Meteora DAMM v2 pool and is locked forever.
- After graduation, the pool fee is 1% and the creator receives 0.6% to 0.7% of volume including the LP rebate.
- An optional anti-sniper fee starts at 25% and decays to about 1% over 60 seconds. It reduces the early sniping advantage and does not stop bots.
- The [Highbar Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oioebamfkpahdnjkbbmldepkackabhed) can launch and trade tokens from X. Extension trades add a 0.02% fee.

Highbar has no holder-reward split and a much smaller audience than pump.fun. Our [Solana launchpad comparison](/articles/pump-fun-alternatives-compare-solana-launchpads) puts more options side by side.

## Which should you pick?

- **Boop.fun** if you want holders, not the deployer, to receive most post-graduation fees, or you want to launch by tagging a bot on X.
- **Pump.fun** if you want the largest audience and a simple creator fee, and your traders accept the in-app Platform Fee.
- **Highbar** if you want a published creator share, a graduation target you choose and locked DAMM v2 liquidity.
