Searching for the best Solana launchpad usually returns a list of names. A more useful search is a list of rules. This checklist tells you which official line to read before you launch or trade, using pages checked on 16 November 2026. It does not pick a winner. For a snapshot of how people already compare these products, see [best Solana launchpad 2026](/articles/best-solana-launchpad-2026) and [pump.fun alternatives](/articles/pump-fun-alternatives-compare-solana-launchpads), then go back to the official docs those articles cite.

## 1. Quote asset

Decide whether the coin should pair with SOL, USDC, JUP, a tokenized stock, or another quote. Highbar is SOL only. Jupiter Studio meme mode is USDC; custom mode allows USDC, SOL or JUP. StonkFun's official pages describe many quotes, including tokenized stocks, on Raydium LaunchLab. Bags documents custom quotes, including supported stock tokens, on a separate official path. If the quote *is* the product, most "alternatives" drop out immediately.

## 2. Program and venue after the threshold

A launchpad is often a front end on someone else's program. Official examples:

- Pump.fun uses its own curve and graduates to PumpSwap.
- Highbar and Jupiter Studio use a Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve and graduate to Meteora DAMM v2.
- Raydium LaunchLab, including official LetsBonk configs on that program, follows a Raydium path, usually into a CPMM pool.
- Heaven uses its own AMM from the first trade and does not migrate.
- Moonshot Create bonds at a $1 million market cap, per official support pages, and points at Meteora DBC for background.

If you care where the coin will trade next month, this line matters more than the brand on the create button.

## 3. The fee you will actually pay

Read the confirmation screen, not a remembered headline. Official tables differ in kind, not only in size:

- Pump.fun: 1.25% on the curve, plus a 0.45% or 0.40% Platform Fee in official apps only.
- Highbar: 1% on the curve and 1% in DAMM v2, no extra website fee, 0.02% on the [Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oioebamfkpahdnjkbbmldepkackabhed).
- Jupiter Studio: 1% or 2% for life; an optional anti-sniper window can start at 99%.
- Moonshot official app: 2.5% or 1% by trade size, on top of a 0.5% / 0.3% coin fee.
- Heaven: 1% protocol fee below $100k market cap, then 0.25% or 0.5%, plus a creator fee.
- Bags Default: 2%, with other official modes from 0.25% to 10%.

A lower creator share is not automatically better for traders, and a lower trader fee is not automatically better for creators. Write down which side you are on.

## 4. Graduation or bonding, and liquidity

Ask when the threshold hits and what happens to liquidity then. Highbar publishes 85, 150 or 250 SOL and locks DAMM v2 liquidity forever. Jupiter Studio's current configs also lock all graduated LP; some older configs let 50% unlock after a year. Float's official docs describe Raydium Burn & Earn locks. Heaven uses virtual SOL and does not migrate. Official pump.fun fee pages used here do not state a graduation-threshold number, so do not invent one. Locked liquidity does not make a token safe.

## 5. Creator share, vesting, reviews and early-buy rules

Creators should read the official share, whether it starts at launch or at graduation, and whether a review can change it. Highbar pays on the curve and after graduation by tier. Studio can vest up to 80% of supply from graduation. Heaven can hold a 1% creator fee during a review and warns that a "Creator" tag is not a safety approval. Boop's official post-graduation table sends 62.5% of a 2% fee to holders or delegates. Anti-sniper fees, where they exist, make early buys more expensive and do not stop bots. For pre-trade checks, use [how to check a Solana token before buying](/articles/how-to-check-a-solana-token-before-buying).

## Where Highbar belongs on this list

Highbar, per the [docs](/docs), is a match only if you wanted a SOL-paired Meteora launch, a chosen SOL tier, a published 1% fee, a creator share on both phases, and locked DAMM v2 liquidity, and you accept a small, new venue. If any earlier line pointed somewhere else, use that product's official page instead. The [launch form](/launch) is the last step, not the first.

Figures in this article were checked on 16 November 2026 against the official pages linked above.

Those pages change, so re-read them before you launch or trade, and do not treat this guide as a substitute for the live docs.

If a figure here disagrees with an official page, the official page is the one that binds.

Highbar is a small, new launchpad. Nothing in this article is a reason to buy or sell a token, and no launchpad can guarantee demand.

Locked liquidity and a creator fee share are design choices. They do not make a token safe, and they do not stop sniper bots on their own.

Always confirm fee rates, graduation targets and liquidity rules on the live docs, or on the on-chain config, immediately before you sign.

A useful way to use a comparison like this is to write down the three rules you actually care about — the curve fee, the creator share, and what happens to liquidity at graduation — and check those three lines on each official page, rather than choosing a launchpad by name or by a social-media clip.

If you are launching, prepare the token name, ticker, image and any links first, then read the fee table for the exact config you are about to sign, including whether an anti-sniper or app fee applies. If you are trading, check the mint, the pool, and the live fee on the confirmation screen.

None of the platforms compared here is affiliated with Highbar, and a factual comparison is not a recommendation to use one of them, or to buy any token that has already launched.

Take your time, read the confirmation screen, and only sign if the live fee and destination pool match what you expected to pay.
