    Methodology

    How every figure on this page is computed.

    Exact figures are read from transaction meta: balance changes, fees and tips as they landed on chain. Derived figures are computed from the pool config, such as the DBC fee split between Meteora, the creator, the platform and referrers.

    The DBC trading fee is split by the pool config. Meteora takes its protocol share first, a referral share of that comes back to referrers on trades made through the highbar UI, and the creator and the platform split the rest by tier. Totals are summed per transaction, so these cards link here instead of a single Solscan page.

    The platform share is used 40% for buyback and burn, 50% for operations and 10% for the treasury. Buckets are pooled per closed period, operations and treasury stay in the fee receiver wallet. LP fees after migration come from a permanently locked DAMM v2 position.

    Tips go to block engines, not highbar. Sniper wallets are those landing a buy within two slots of the pool being created. Network fees are split between validators and the burned part of the base fee. Windows are calendar days in UTC.

    Overview

    highbar is a Solana token launchpad built on Meteora. Tokens trade on a Dynamic Bonding Curve (DBC), then graduate into a DAMM v2 pool with locked liquidity.

    Not an investment. $HIGHER is not an investment and promises no returns.
    {01}Launch a tokenName it, pick a tier, go live. {02}Open BookEvery fee, reconciled against chain. {03}GuidesShort answers to common questions.

    Launching a token

    1. Identity. Name (up to 32 bytes), ticker (A to Z and 0 to 9, up to 10 bytes), image up to 2 MB, plus optional links.
    2. Tier. Standard, Higher or Max. This sets the graduation target and creator fee.
    3. Protection. Optionally switch on anti-sniper and make a dev buy in Block 0.
    4. Launch. Review the fee summary, then confirm in your wallet.

    Creator fees are claimed from the Portfolio page. Cashback accrues at 0.05% of volume per UTC day, minimum payout 0.001 SOL.

    Anti-sniper

    When enabled, the fee starts at 25% and decays to about 1% within 60 seconds, so early bots pay for speed. When off, every buyer pays the normal 1% fee.

    Dev lock

    Creators can lock their tokens with Jupiter Lock. Locks and unlock dates are public on the Locks page.

    Private launch

    Private launch separates the person from the token. It is optional and off by default. Each option works on its own.

    1. Hidden creator wallet. The token is created from a fresh one-time wallet, so the creator shown on-chain is not your main wallet.
    2. Private payment. The launch wallet is funded through a shielded transfer, so the funding cannot be traced back to you.
    3. Stealth fee claims. Creator fees are paid out to a new address on each claim.

    Dev lock and the Open Book still work. Locks and fee totals stay public, only the link to your identity is hidden.

    Migration

    When a token reaches its tier target it migrates from the DBC to a Meteora DAMM v2 pool. Liquidity is locked, and LP fees continue to flow to the creator, platform and Meteora.

    Rewards & $HIGHER

    Cashback, referrals, creator milestones and $HIGHER holder perks live under Rewards. Part of platform fees will buy back and burn $HIGHER, with every burn recorded in the Open Book.
